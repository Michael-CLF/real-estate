Important commission question

What if you find the buyer before your agent does?

Under a genuine open listing, a broker is generally compensated when that broker successfully produces the buyer. If you find the buyer independently, you may not owe a listing commission.

However, an exclusive-right-to-sell agreement, an agent’s prior involvement, a protection period or other contract language may produce a different result. Always review the agreement you signed before assuming that no commission is owed.
