1. Scope of this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information processed by Expertise Holdings LLC, doing business as NavStreet (“NavStreet,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), through the NavStreet website, applications, marketplace, and related services (collectively, the “Service”).

The Service currently focuses on real-estate activity in North Carolina. As NavStreet expands, this Policy will continue to apply unless a state-specific or service-specific notice states otherwise.

This Policy does not govern third-party websites, applications, or services that operate under their own privacy policies.

2. Information we collect

Account and contact information

We may collect:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Account identifiers
  • Profile photograph
  • Account status, role, permissions, and verification status

Property and listing information

Sellers may provide:

  • Property addresses and location information
  • Prices, descriptions, photographs, property characteristics, features, amenities, and condition information
  • Homeowners-association, tax, parcel, school, utility, construction, and accessibility information
  • Showing availability and listing status
  • Seller certifications and publication records

Information intentionally included in a published listing is publicly available. Public listing information may be viewed, copied, shared, indexed, or retained by others outside NavStreet.

Buyer and transaction information

We may collect information associated with:

  • Saved properties and favorites
  • Property inquiries and messages
  • Showing requests, scheduling, and responses
  • Offers, counteroffers, withdrawals, acceptances, and related versions
  • Proposed and accepted transaction terms
  • Earnest-money, due-diligence, inspection, appraisal, financing, insurance, title, walkthrough, and closing dates
  • Transaction tasks, statuses, notes, reminders, and activity history
  • Documents uploaded or generated in connection with a transaction

Professional and business information

Professionals and businesses may provide business names, professional names, categories, licensing information, credentials, service areas, addresses, contact information, descriptions, websites, photographs, and other directory-profile information.

Payment and promotion information

We may collect transaction identifiers, payment status, amounts, purchased services, promotion-code usage, discounts, refunds, and related transaction records.

Payment-card information is processed by Stripe. NavStreet does not intentionally store complete payment-card numbers.

Technical and usage information

We and our service providers may automatically collect:

  • Internet Protocol address
  • Browser, device, and operating-system information
  • Referring pages and marketing-source information
  • Pages viewed and features used
  • Listing views, interactions, and session identifiers
  • Approximate location derived from an Internet Protocol address
  • Dates, times, error records, security events, and system activity
  • Cookies, local storage, and similar technologies

Communications

We may collect messages, inquiries, support requests, feedback, notices, and other communications sent through or concerning the Service.

We may receive personal information:

  • Enable inquiries, showings, saved properties, offers, and transaction tools
  • Process payments, promotions, refunds, and service upgrades

  • Send account, security, listing, inquiry, showing, offer, payment, transaction, and legal communications
  • Measure traffic, marketing sources, engagement, and Service performance
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, misuse, security threats, and violations of our Terms
  • Debug, maintain, protect, and improve the Service

5. How we disclose information

Public listing information

Property information, photographs, prices, descriptions, listing statuses, and other information included in a published listing may be displayed publicly and shared through links, social media, email, or QR codes.

Buyers and sellers

When a user submits an inquiry, showing request, or offer, NavStreet may share information reasonably necessary for the recipient to respond and participate in the requested activity.

For example, a buyer’s name, email address, telephone number, message, proposed showing time, or offer information may be shared with the applicable seller. Relevant seller and property information may be shared with the applicable buyer.

Service providers

We may disclose information to companies that provide services on our behalf, including:

  • Google Firebase for authentication, databases, file storage, application functions, and related infrastructure
  • Stripe for payment processing, fraud prevention, and identity verification
  • SendGrid for transactional email delivery
  • Google Analytics for traffic, acquisition, and usage measurement when enabled
  • Hosting, security, support, document, communications, and operational service providers

Service providers may process information only as permitted by their agreements, applicable law, and their own privacy obligations.

Legal and safety disclosures

We may disclose information when we reasonably believe disclosure is necessary to:

  • Comply with a subpoena, court order, warrant, legal process, or applicable law
  • Respond to lawful requests from government authorities
  • Protect users, NavStreet, Expertise Holdings LLC, or another person
  • Investigate fraud, security incidents, illegal conduct, or violations of our Terms
  • Establish, exercise, or defend legal claims

Business transactions

Information may be transferred in connection with an actual or proposed merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, bankruptcy, or transfer of the Service, subject to appropriate confidentiality and legal requirements.

No sale of personal information

NavStreet does not sell personal information for monetary consideration.

If our practices change in a manner that constitutes a sale or regulated sharing under applicable law, we will update this Policy and provide any legally required choices before applying the changed practice.

6. Identity verification

NavStreet may require identity verification for certain activities. Identity verification is currently provided through Stripe Identity.

Depending on the verification method, Stripe may collect government-issued identification, photographs, selfies, biometric identifiers, information entered during verification, device information, and fraud-prevention information.

Stripe processes identity-verification information under its own privacy policy and may act as an independent controller of certain information. NavStreet may receive verification status, verification results, and limited information made available through Stripe’s services.

Do not send government identification or sensitive identity documents to NavStreet by ordinary email.

7. Cookies, local storage, and analytics

NavStreet may use cookies, browser storage, session identifiers, and similar technologies to:

  • Maintain authentication and security
  • Preserve application preferences and workflow progress
  • Prevent duplicate listing-view counts
  • Measure traffic and Service performance
  • Identify marketing sources through referral and campaign parameters
  • Diagnose errors and prevent abuse

When Google Analytics is enabled, it may collect information concerning page views, sessions, devices, approximate location, referral sources, and interactions with the Service.

Browser settings may allow you to block or remove cookies. Disabling necessary storage may prevent authentication or other Service features from functioning correctly.

8. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide and secure the Service
  • Maintain listing and transaction history
  • Complete payments and maintain financial records
  • Resolve disputes and enforce agreements
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Satisfy legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations

Retention periods vary according to the nature of the information, the status of the account or transaction, legal requirements, and operational needs.

Some records may remain in backups, security logs, transaction histories, or legally required archives after an account or public listing is removed.

9. Data security

NavStreet uses administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. These safeguards include authentication, authorization controls, database security rules, server-side validation, encrypted network transmission, restricted administrative access, and service-provider security controls.

No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. You are responsible for protecting access to your email account, devices, and NavStreet verification codes.

Contact legal@navstreet.com if you believe your NavStreet account or personal information has been compromised.
