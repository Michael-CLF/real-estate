How Much Does a Real Estate Listing Agent Cost?

Real estate commissions can represent a significant expense when selling a home. The amount a homeowner pays depends on the property's sale price and the compensation negotiated with their real estate agent.

This NavStreet analysis compares hypothetical listing-agent commissions of 1.5%, 2%, 2.5% and 3% with NavStreet's $49 standard for-sale-by-owner listing fee.

The analysis illustrates potential differences in listing expenses. It does not measure actual savings achieved by homeowners who sold without an agent.

Research Methodology and Data Sources

Housing market data

This analysis uses reported state-level median home sale prices for October 2026. Redfin's Housing Market Tracker is the primary source, with supplemental Realtor.com data for Louisiana.

  • Redfin Data Center — Primary housing market dataset.
  • Redfin Research Methodology — Housing data methodology.
  • Realtor.com Louisiana Housing Market — Supplemental median sold price.

How potential savings are calculated

Potential listing-fee savings

(Median home sale price × illustrative listing-agent commission rate) − $49

For example, a homeowner selling a $400,000 property under an illustrative 3% listing-agent commission scenario would incur a $12,000 listing-agent commission.

Compared with NavStreet's $49 standard listing fee, the potential difference in listing expenses would be $11,951.

Research limitations

  • Real estate commissions are negotiable and are not established by law.
  • Commission percentages are hypothetical scenarios, not measured state commission rates.
  • Median sale prices represent market-level statistics, not individual property valuations.
  • Buyer-agent compensation, closing costs, attorney fees, optional listing enhancements and other expenses are excluded.
  • The average state-level savings figure is unweighted and is not a national transaction-weighted savings estimate.
  • Louisiana's supplemental data may differ in methodology from the primary Redfin dataset.
  • Nationwide research coverage does not imply that NavStreet listing services are available in every state.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much can I save selling my house without a Realtor?

Potential savings depend on your home's sale price, the listing-agent commission you would otherwise negotiate and the expenses associated with selling independently.

Are real estate commission rates different in every state?

Real estate commissions are negotiable. Compensation arrangements can vary by agent, brokerage, property and transaction. This report does not establish or estimate prevailing commission rates by state.

Do I have to pay a buyer's agent when selling my home by owner?

Buyer-agent compensation depends on the agreements and negotiations involved in the transaction. Sellers may agree to contribute toward buyer-agent compensation. These expenses are not included in this analysis.

Does selling a home by owner eliminate closing costs?

No. Sellers may still incur title expenses, transfer taxes, attorney fees, settlement charges and other transaction-related expenses.
