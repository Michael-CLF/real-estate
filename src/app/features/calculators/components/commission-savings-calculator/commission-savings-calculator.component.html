How Much Can You Save Selling Your House Without a Realtor?

Selling a house for sale by owner, commonly referred to as FSBO, allows homeowners to advertise and manage the sale of their property without hiring a listing agent.

One potential financial benefit of selling your home independently is avoiding a percentage-based listing-agent commission. The amount you could save depends on your home's sale price, negotiated commissions, buyer-agent compensation and additional selling expenses.

NavStreet offers homeowners a standard property advertising listing for $49, along with tools designed to help sellers market their homes and connect directly with prospective buyers.

How Real Estate Commission Savings Are Calculated

Our real estate commission savings calculator compares estimated agent-assisted selling expenses with estimated for-sale-by-owner advertising and compensation expenses.

Agent-Assisted Selling Expenses

For Sale by Owner Expenses

Potential savings = Agent-assisted expenses − FSBO expenses

Frequently Asked Questions

How much commission can I save selling my house for sale by owner?

Your potential savings depend on the listing-agent commission you would otherwise negotiate and the expenses associated with selling independently. For example, a hypothetical 3% listing commission on a $400,000 home would equal $12,000.

Are real estate commissions negotiable?

Yes. Real estate commissions are negotiable. There is no universally required commission rate. Homeowners should compare available services, compensation arrangements and selling expenses.

Do FSBO sellers have to pay a buyer's agent?

Buyer-agent compensation depends on the agreements and negotiations involved in the transaction. Our calculator allows you to enter the amount you anticipate paying, including zero.

Does NavStreet charge a real estate commission?

NavStreet charges $49 for a standard property advertising listing rather than a percentage-based listing-agent commission. Optional listing enhancements may cost extra.

Does selling FSBO eliminate closing costs?

No. Homeowners may still incur closing costs, attorney fees, title expenses, taxes, negotiated compensation and other transaction-related costs.

Does the NavStreet listing include MLS placement?

No. NavStreet is an independent real estate advertising platform. A standard NavStreet listing is not an MLS listing.
