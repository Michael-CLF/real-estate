What happens next

Offer-expiration behavior

  1. 1
    Offer submitted

    The seller receives the complete offer and its expiration deadline.

  2. 2
    Seller reviews

    The seller may accept, reject or create a counteroffer while the offer remains available.

  3. 3
    Deadline reached

    If no acceptance has been completed, NavStreet records the offer as expired and preserves it in the historical record.

Expiration does not delete the offer

The offer and every related version remain available to the buyer and seller as part of the permanent offer history.
